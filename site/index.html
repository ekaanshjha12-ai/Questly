How it works

Three steps, and the rest is the game.

Set a goal

Pass an exam, run a half marathon, finish the portfolio. Each goal hands out its own quests — four a day, one a week, three a month — written for what you are actually going for, and for your age.

Do the work

Start a quest and get on with it. Use the timer if you like: Questly runs the clock on its own server, and time counted that way is never capped. Anything you only tell it you did counts toward a daily limit.

Level up

XP, coins, ranks and gear your character actually wears. Your base changes with the hour, and your club's hall grows from a workshop into a landmark as everyone shows up.

What's inside

A whole game built around getting things done.

Quest Board

Daily, weekly, monthly and optional quests with real states — in progress, locked, expired — each levelled by how long it takes.

Timer

A countdown or a stopwatch for anything, with steps for the session and ambient sound. Timed on the server, so its XP is never self-reported.

Duels

Challenge another player on focus minutes or daily check-ins. The server settles it. XP and bragging rights only — never money.

Clubs

Entry trials, club challenges, chat and Club XP — with a hall on the map that grows as the club does.

World map

Nine regions with weekly quests and monthly events, lit for your time of day and locked until you have earned them.

Adventure Log

Share a finished quest, a duel result or a focus session. Appreciation, comments, and a moderation queue behind it all.

Study and habits

Flashcards, a coach that makes you explain it back, a habit grid, moods, and a planner you can drag a week around in.

AI planner

Turn a goal into a dated plan in a few minutes, then put the whole thing on your board in one go.

Fair play

Earned, not claimed.

A game about doing the work only means something if the work is what counts. These are the rules Questly holds itself to, and they are enforced on the server rather than asked of you.

XP comes from what happened

Focus minutes are timed by the server, every reward is paid once, and anything you simply tell Questly you did counts against a daily ceiling. Nothing can be typed straight into your level.

No stakes, ever

No gambling, betting or money on the line — anywhere. Duels and club challenges are worth XP, standing and recognition, and the strongest consequence a club can set is a warning or losing your place in it.

Written for your age

Questly is for 15 and up. Quests for players under 18 keep to school, pocket money and healthy habits, an under-18 can close contact from adults entirely, and phone numbers, links and risky messages are stopped in chat.

Your data is yours

No ads, and nothing sold. Download everything Questly holds about you whenever you like, or delete the account for good — it takes your posts, messages, duels and progress with it.

Questions

Before you start.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, nothing to buy with real money, and no paid tier holding features back.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Questly runs in your browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen, and it opens like an app from then on.

How old do you have to be?

Fifteen. Your date of birth is asked at sign-up, shapes the quests you are given, and cannot be changed afterwards.

What if I forget my password?

You are given a recovery code when you sign up — keep it somewhere safe. It sets a new password, works once, and is replaced with a fresh one each time it is used.

Does it work offline?

Questly opens offline and shows the progress it last saved. New quests, XP and anything social need a connection.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, in Settings → Account and data, with your password. Download your data first if you want to keep a copy.
