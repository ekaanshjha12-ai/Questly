> Questly is a place for people working on something real. Cheer each other on, compete fairly, and keep everyone safe — including the youngest players here, who are {{minimumAge}}.

## Be someone people are glad to meet

- Encourage people. Appreciate progress, and keep criticism kind and useful.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Respect a "no". If someone declines a duel, ignores a message request or blocks you, leave it there.

## Keep it real

- Share your real progress. Do not fake proof, check-ins or results.
- Earn XP, levels and Club XP by doing the work. Using bugs, scripts, multiple accounts or other tricks to get ahead is not allowed — report bugs instead.
- Be yourself. Do not pretend to be another person, a brand or the Questly team.

## Never allowed

- **Harassment and bullying**, including repeated unwanted contact, insults, pile-ons and mocking someone's progress.
- **Hate**: attacking people for who they are, including race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender identity or sexual orientation.
- **Threats and violence**, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
- **Sexual content of any kind**, and anything that sexualises or exploits a young person. This is never tolerated and may be reported to the authorities.
- **Dangerous challenges**: nothing that risks anyone's health or safety, including extreme fasting, sleep deprivation or physical stunts.
- **Illegal activity**, including selling drugs, weapons or other restricted goods.
- **Gambling, betting and money**: no wagers on duels or challenges, no selling or buying accounts, items or XP, and no asking for money.
- **Spam and scams**: repeated posts, advertising, fake giveaways and suspicious links.
- **Private information**: sharing someone else's personal details, photos or messages without their permission.
- **Moving people off Questly**: asking for or sharing phone numbers, emails, social media or other apps in messages and comments.
- **Content you do not have the right to share**, such as other people's work.

## Clubs

- Club rules, trials and challenges must fit these guidelines.
- Mandatory club challenges should be safe and achievable. Leaders cannot demand money, payments or anything outside Questly.
- Owners and officers can remove posts in their club and members who break the club's rules — use that power fairly.

## Duels

- Duels are voluntary and should be good for both people.
- Agree terms that are safe. Focus time and daily check-ins are the point; nothing about a duel should put anyone at risk.

## What happens when rules are broken

- Some things are blocked automatically, like abusive words or contact details in messages.
- Reports go to the Questly team, who review them and may remove content, remove XP gained unfairly, suspend or close accounts.
- Serious harm, especially to a young person, may be reported to the authorities.

## Report it

Use **Report** on a player's card, a post, a comment, a conversation, a duel or a club. It sends a copy of what was said to the Questly team. You can block the person too — they will not be told.

See the [Safety Centre](/legal/safety) for more on staying safe.
