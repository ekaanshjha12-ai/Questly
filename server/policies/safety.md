## Tools you have

**Block.** Open someone's card and choose **Block**. You will not see each other, they cannot message you or send you duel offers, and anything you had going together ends. They are not told.

**Report.** Use **Report** on a player's card, a post, a comment, a conversation, a duel or a club. The Questly team gets a copy of what was said, even if it is later deleted. The person you report is not told who reported them.

**Message requests.** When someone new writes to you, it arrives as a request. Nothing they send reaches your chats unless you accept or reply.

**Contact from adults.** If you are under 18, choose in **Settings** whether players who are 18 or older can message you or send you duels.

**Duel offers.** In **Settings**, you can stop anyone from sending you duel offers.

**Leaderboard.** Choose **hide me** on the leaderboard to leave it at any time.

## Staying safe

- Keep personal details private: your address, school, phone number and other accounts.
- Be wary of anyone who wants to move the conversation to another app, asks for photos, or asks you to keep things secret. Block and report them.
- A duel or club challenge should never hurt you. Stop if it does.
- If something online is upsetting you, talk to someone you trust.

## What happens after a report

The Questly team reviews reports and can remove content, remove unfairly earned progress, suspend or close accounts. If you reported something, you will get a notification when your report has been reviewed.
